## Description (4000)

FitMovie turns your action and dive clips into videos that show what it really took. Load the FIT file from your sports watch and FitMovie puts your heart rate, pace, speed, distance or – for dives – depth and water temperature on the video, perfectly in sync.

DATA OVERLAY
• Heart rate, pace, speed, distance, elevation, temperature and elapsed time
• Dive mode: depth, water temperature and a live depth profile
• Choose the metrics, corner, layout, size, colour and units (metric or imperial)
• Optional profile graph with a moving marker
• Syncs automatically with the video's recording time, with a fine offset when you need it

EDIT
• Trim the start and end, or cut out any part in the middle
• Stabilize shaky footage, with adjustable smoothness and crop
• Filters: Extra Vivid, Black & White, Noir, Sepia, Chrome, Fade, Instant, Warm, Cool and Underwater Fix, which brings back the reds lost under water
• Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and vignette
• Add background music with volume, looping and fades

YOUR FOOTAGE
• Works with video from iPhone, action cameras, drones and Android phones (MOV and MP4, HEVC and H.264)
• Open FIT files from Files, or share them straight from your watch's app
• Export in HEVC or H.264, up to 4K, then save to Photos or share

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
FitMovie works entirely on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no tracking.

## App Review notes

FitMovie overlays data from FIT activity files (the standard export format of sports watches) on videos.

To test the overlay:
1. Download a sample FIT file in Safari and save it to Files:
   - Run: https://github.com/petervanmanen/fitmovie/raw/main/docs/appstore/samples/sample-run.fit
   - Dive: https://github.com/petervanmanen/fitmovie/raw/main/docs/appstore/samples/sample-dive.fit
2. In FitMovie, tap "Choose from Photos" and pick any video.
3. Open the "Data" tab, tap "Load FIT File…" and choose the sample from Files.
4. Your test video was not recorded during the sample activity, so tap "Align to Activity Start" in the "Sync with video" section. The heart rate, pace (run) or depth (dive) then appear on the video.

Other features (trim, stabilize, filters, music, export) work with any video. No account or login is needed.
