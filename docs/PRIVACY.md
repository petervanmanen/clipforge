# FitMovie Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

FitMovie is a video editor for iPhone and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## The short version

FitMovie does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

## Your videos, music and activity files

- FitMovie opens the videos, music and FIT activity files that you choose, from Photos, Files or another app.
- These files are processed only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
- Copies the app makes while you edit are kept in the app's temporary storage on your device. iOS can remove them at any time.

## Photos

- FitMovie only reads the videos you pick in the system photo picker. It does not get access to the rest of your library.
- If you tap **Save to Photos**, FitMovie asks for permission to *add* your edited video to your library. It cannot read or change anything else there.

## No accounts, tracking or analytics

FitMovie has no accounts. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it makes no network connections of its own.

## Sharing

When you use **Share**, the edited video goes only where you send it, using the iOS share sheet. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

## Children

FitMovie does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
